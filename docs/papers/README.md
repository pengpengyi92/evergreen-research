## Conventions

- **Snapshot, not oracle**: every corpus number in a paper is a claim about a
  dated snapshot of `data/papers.jsonl`; the live corpus grows weekly.
- **Markdown is the living source**: PDFs are compiled snapshots; venue-targeted
  builds happen at submission time.
- **License**: CC BY 4.0 (text) — reuse it, cite us.
- **Human review gate**: these are drafts. Before any arXiv submission, each
  paper requires ≥2 human reviewers (see the repo's
  [`LAUNCH.md`](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-research/blob/main/LAUNCH.md))
  and must pass `presearch audit`.

## arXiv intent

Paper 1 (the living survey) is the flagship; these two are the companion system
papers. Submission is blocked on arXiv endorsement for first-time authors — the
repo, digests, and discussions are the publication artifact until then. Open a
discussion if you want to review, challenge, or co-author.
