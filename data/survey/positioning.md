## Differentiation in one paragraph

Existing surveys on LLM reasoning and test-time compute are static, single-team
snapshots frozen at publication, and they treat test-time compute in isolation.
This survey is generated by a **reproducible, continuously-updating corpus
pipeline** — code on GitHub, an append-only `data/papers.jsonl`, deterministic
structuring, and an auditable evidence chain from every arXiv record to every
survey claim — so the survey accumulates evidence instead of rotting. It adds a
**compute-allocation lens**: one resource (compute) distributed across train
vs. test time, model vs. environment, and representation vs. reward, letting
the six pillars be read as points on a single tradeoff curve.
